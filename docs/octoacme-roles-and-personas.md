# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Quality Assurance Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Quality Assurance Leads own the testing strategy and quality standards. They collaborate with product and development teams to define acceptance criteria, design test plans, and validate that solutions meet quality and compliance requirements.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy and acceptance criteria validation approach
- Create and maintain test plans and test cases
- Execute manual and automated testing
- Track quality metrics and defects
- Validate features meet acceptance criteria before release
- Identify quality risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Ensure high-quality deliverables that meet user expectations
- Reduce production defects through comprehensive testing
- Enable fast, confident releases with measurable quality signals

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and acceptance criteria reviews
- Daily standups and test status updates
- QA sign-off before release
- Defect reports and quality metrics dashboards

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Developers**: Collaborate on test coverage, automation frameworks, and defect resolution
- **Product Managers**: Align on acceptance criteria and quality expectations
- **Project Managers**: Report on quality metrics and testing progress against timelines
- **Technical Lead/Architect**: Coordinate on testability requirements and quality standards
- **UX/Design Lead**: Validate user experience quality and usability requirements

---

## Technical Lead/Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads provide architectural guidance, make key technical decisions, and ensure solutions are scalable, maintainable, and aligned with long-term technical strategy.

### Responsibilities
- Guide architectural design and technical approach
- Identify and mitigate technical risks and dependencies
- Ensure code quality and maintainability standards
- Mentor developers and review technical designs
- Coordinate integration points with other systems
- Validate solutions meet performance and scalability requirements

### Goals
- Deliver architecturally sound, scalable solutions
- Reduce technical debt and risk
- Enable rapid iteration without compromising long-term health

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture discussions
- Code reviews and pull request feedback
- Risk mitigation planning in weekly syncs
- Performance and scalability validation reports

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Developers**: Mentor on technical best practices, review designs and code quality
- **Project Managers**: Escalate technical risks and dependencies affecting schedules
- **QA/Quality Assurance Lead**: Define testability requirements and performance benchmarks
- **Stakeholder/Sponsor**: Communicate technical trade-offs and long-term strategy implications
- **UX/Design Lead**: Advise on technical feasibility of design requirements

---

## Stakeholder/Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and Sponsors provide business context, strategic direction, and approval authority. They ensure the project aligns with organizational priorities and remove business-level obstacles to success.

### Responsibilities
- Provide business context and strategic alignment for projects
- Approve key decisions and resource commitments
- Remove organizational and business-level blockers
- Communicate project value and priorities to leadership
- Provide feedback on outcomes and impact
- Manage stakeholder expectations and visibility

### Goals
- Ensure projects deliver measurable business value
- Maintain organizational alignment and strategic fit
- Enable teams to move quickly with clear authorization

### Typical Communication
- Milestone and release approvals
- Monthly stakeholder status updates
- Escalation path for business-level blockers
- Post-project outcome reviews and lessons learned

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Project Managers**: Provide strategic context, approve timeline trade-offs, escalate blockers
- **Product Managers**: Align on business outcomes, prioritize features based on strategic goals
- **Developers**: Communicate business rationale and user value behind technical work
- **Technical Lead/Architect**: Approve significant technical trade-offs with business impact

---

## Scrum Master/Agile Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters and Agile Coaches facilitate agile ceremonies, coach teams on agile practices, and remove organizational impediments. They enable self-organizing teams to maintain momentum and continuous improvement.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate sprint planning, daily standups, reviews, and retrospectives
- Coach teams on agile principles, practices, and mindsets
- Identify and help remove organizational and team impediments
- Maintain project board hygiene and workflow visibility
- Foster psychological safety and team engagement
- Mentor other team members on agile practices

### Goals
- Enable team self-organization and ownership
- Maintain consistent sprint velocity and delivery rhythm
- Foster continuous improvement culture
- Reduce friction and remove blockers quickly

### Typical Communication
- Sprint ceremonies (daily standups, planning, reviews, retrospectives)
- Impediment tracking and escalation
- One-on-one coaching and feedback
- Retrospective action items and follow-up

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Developers**: Coach on agile practices, facilitate daily coordination
- **Project Managers**: Collaborate on sprint planning and timeline management
- **Product Managers**: Facilitate backlog refinement and prioritization ceremonies
- **All Roles**: Foster psychological safety and encourage feedback across the team

---

## UX/Design Lead

### Role Summary
UX/Design Leads ensure user-centered design and usability. They own the user experience vision, define acceptance criteria for user-facing work, and validate that solutions deliver excellent usability.

### Responsibilities
- Define user experience requirements and design standards
- Create wireframes, prototypes, and design specifications
- Own acceptance criteria for user-facing features
- Conduct user research and usability testing
- Validate implementation matches design intent
- Identify usability risks and propose improvements

### Goals
- Deliver exceptional user experiences that meet customer needs
- Ensure consistency and coherence across the product
- Reduce usability issues and user frustration
- Validate design through user research and testing

### Typical Communication
- Design specifications and acceptance criteria
- User research findings and insights
- Usability testing results and feedback
- Design review and feedback sessions

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Product Managers**: Collaborate on user research, user stories, and feature prioritization
- **Developers**: Partner on implementation feasibility, animation details, and responsive design
- **QA/Quality Assurance Lead**: Define usability test cases and user experience acceptance criteria
- **Stakeholder/Sponsor**: Communicate user value and design decisions affecting features
- **Technical Lead/Architect**: Discuss technical feasibility of design approaches
